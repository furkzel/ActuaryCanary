# 0408 — The what-if of MT21, the page of M08 and its finding rules

## Context
Stage 5 of F2a adds the what-if (MT21 `programme`), the M08 page in English and Turkish and the finding rules of the page (RP01 1.5.0).

## Decisions
- **Alternative programmes** are entries of the assumptions register with method `MT21`: `scope` names the programme, `target` the treaty_id, `name` a field of reinsurance_treaties and `value` its value (`line` may list lines separated by commas). A treaty without inception and expiry dates takes the contract periods of the loaded programme. The rows pass the same checks as the loaded treaties, so a treaty without its terms is left out and named in `left_out`. No new file format; the register is read as before (`--assumptions`).
- **Settings** (`reinsurance`, part of the run id): `programme` loaded (default), alternative or both; `loss_view` ultimate (default) or incurred; `inuring_order` CV09 only; `simulate` false only — **not built**: the parameter exists, a true value is refused with that reason (planner decision 4).
- **programme_comparison.csv**, one row per programme: treaties, the treaties left out, the loss view used, gross loss, expected ceded loss, net loss, ceded premium, commission, reinstatement premium, the reinsurance result (ceded loss + commission − ceded premium − reinstatement premium) and the volatility ratio of RI05 (mean over the lines where it is computed, with their number). Ultimate view: each claim's incurred at the cut-off times its origin's selected ultimate over incurred (MT21 assumption, IBNR spread pro rata); without a selection the incurred view is used and noted. Gross figures are the same in every row.
- **Page** (anchor `reinsurance`, template `reinsurance.html.j2`): headline (ceded share of written premium and of incurred claims from RI01, the reinsurance result of the last three calendar years from RI02, the index clause in use); the treaty table (type, terms, index clause in use, RI03 claims and premium shares, computed and reported ceded incurred with a difference marked, claims or events hit, units exhausted, reinstatements used); gross, ceded and net loss ratios by origin with RI04; the what-if beside RI05 by line; allocation notes (amounts and parts by allocation method, approximations marked); the reconciliation (count and the first ten treaty years that differ). 60 new labels in `language.yaml`; data notes stay English (record 0224).
- **Finding rules (RP01 1.5.0, scope run, priorities 24 to 26):** KF44 a treaty year whose computed and reported ceded incurred differ beyond the tolerance of record 0405 (no further threshold: one differing treaty year already means the data do not follow the treaty table as read); KF45 a layer exhausted by a claim or an event, its aggregate limit exhausted or its reinstatements used up (a fact of the run, no threshold); KF46 more than `approximate_allocation_share_limit` **0.05** of the booked ceded amounts (absolute) allocated as an aggregate approximation or left unallocated: below five per cent the net figures by line and origin rest almost wholly on given or claim-level amounts; above it a reader must know that part of the net is spread by gross incurred.

## Consequences
- On the tiny reinsurance_heavy run KF44 fires (QS_MOTOR_OD and XL_OD_RISK on the claims with injected extreme payments; the TPL layers because the data follow the occurrence-date clause) and KF45 fires (XL_OD_RISK, XL_TPL_L1 and XL_TPL_L2 of 2022); KF46 does not (every booked amount is given or allocated to policies).
- Tests: `tests/report/test_page_m08.py`, the cases of KF44 to KF46 in `tests/report/test_findings.py`, the what-if in `tests/modules/test_m08_reinsurance.py`.
