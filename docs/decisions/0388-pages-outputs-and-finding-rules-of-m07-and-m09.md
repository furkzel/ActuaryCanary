# 0388 — Wiring, report pages and finding rules of M07 and M09

## Context
Stage 8 of F1bc puts the modules M07 (recoveries) and M09 (expenses) into a run and into the report, in English and Turkish, and adds their finding rules as RP01 1.3.0 (draft).

## Decisions
- **Outputs.** When M07 ran, a run writes `recoveries.csv` (flows per claim, type and month, original currency, with the flag `derived`), `recovery_development.csv` (the RC04 view by segment, line, recovery type, origin and development period, without the lineage columns) and `recovery_reconciliation.csv` (one row per difference between the transactions and a to-date field; only a header when there is none). When M09 ran it writes `expenses_by_period.csv` (month, type, entity, segment, line, currency, rows, amount, amount at value-date prices). All four go through `write_table` and are listed in the manifest after `sensitivity.csv`. M05 and its outputs are untouched.
- **Pages.** `M07 Recoveries` (anchor `recoveries`): the source of the figures; recovered to date by type with its share of paid and what the last development period added against the one before; one heatmap per recovery type of recoveries over paid by origin and age, all lines together (not in the ORIGINAL currency view, where amounts of several currencies are not added); per line the subrogation and salvage ratios of each origin with its development age (RC01, RC02) and the total (RC04); the delay table (RC03); the source note and the reconciliation (the count and the first ten differences). `M09 Expenses` (anchor `expenses`): expense and commission ratio of the last twelve months against the twelve before; both ratios per line with a chart of the expense ratio by line and month; unit costs and the claims-handling ratio by period with the reason where a value is missing; the expenses booked without a line by year, type and the level they carry, with the sentence that they are never spread. Both pages are in the navigation through the list of pages; the templates are `recoveries.html.j2` and `expenses.html.j2`; every label has its Turkish text in `language.yaml` (58 new entries). Notes of measures and of the tables are data of the run and stay English (record 0224), except the fixed notes of the unit costs, which the page shows in the language of the report.
- **Twelve-month windows** need every month of the window in the monthly view; otherwise the cell is empty and the rule does not fire.
- **Finding rules (RP01 1.3.0), all of scope `run`, fixed sentences in both languages, priorities 17 to 23 after the existing rules:**
  - KF35 recoveries to date: fires when anything was recovered; states the amount, the share of paid and the source. Information, no threshold.
  - KF36 recovery transactions differ from the to-date fields: fires on one difference or more (DV-RCV-08); a single difference already means two sources that disagree.
  - KF37 weaker recovery in the latest origin: at the first development age the latest origin has recovered at most half of what the earlier origins (at least two, taken together) had at the same age; `recovery_drop_limit` **0.5**. Recoveries are few and lumpy, so a smaller shortfall says nothing; half is a level the RC04 specification calls an early sign. The sentence says that it rests on few recoveries.
  - KF38 and KF39 the expense ratio and the commission ratio of the last twelve months moved by at least two points of earned premium against the twelve before; `expense_ratio_change_limit` **0.02**, one limit for both. Two points is about a quarter of the expense ratio of the generated portfolios (7 to 12 per cent) and far above the month-to-month noise of a twelve-month ratio (0.2 to 0.3 points on the tiny baseline).
  - KF40 expenses booked without a line: at least a quarter of the expenses of the last twelve months carry no line; `unallocated_expense_share_limit` **0.25**. Below that the ratios by line still describe most of the cost; above it they leave out so much that the reader must know.
  - KF41 unit costs stopped by a month without expense rows: fires when one period or more of EX04 or EX05 is not computed for that reason. No threshold: a missing booking is a fact of the data.
- The facts are computed in `report/recoveries_section.py` and `report/expenses_section.py` from the measure views and the tables of the modules; a fact the run cannot compute is absent, so its rule does not fire.
