# 0403 — Allocation, the CV03 1.1.0 ceded fields, the premium side, and the comparison with the generator's truth

## Context
Stage 3 of F2a assembles the treaty application from a run, allocates booked treaty-level movements (CV09), builds the ceded fields of the net basis where the data have treaty-level movements only (CV03 1.1.0), and compares the application with the generator's truth (records 0240, 0241).

## Decisions
- **Computed application and allocation kept apart.** `modules/reinsurance_inputs.py` builds the claim book (gross paid = paid_to_date, incurred = paid + case_reserve, one row per claim and valuation date), the sections, the price indices, the spot rates (CV01 lookback, direct or reciprocal), the facultative shares and the premium side, and applies the programme at every valuation date held. `convert/allocation.py` spreads booked movements; it uses the application only as weights.
- **Precedence (planner decision 1).** A claim movement with a claim_id and a premium movement with a policy_id are `given`. Only the others are allocated. On data with per-claim ceded fields nothing changes: `ceded_valuation_fields` returns claim_valuations untouched when a ceded field is present, so no measure value moves.
- **Weights.** Claim movements: the claims' computed ceded movement of the same treaty, kind and booking month (paid flow, or reserve change); an event code on the movement keeps that event's claims; weights that cancel out (sum below 1e-9 of their absolute sum) count as none. CEDED_PREMIUM: gross written premium of the month (proportional) or gross premium earned in the month, pro rata by days (non-proportional), of the policies in the treaty's scope, narrowed to the movement's line when it has one. RI_COMMISSION: the weights of its treaty's ceded premium. REINSTATEMENT_PREMIUM: line and origin cells by the ceded incurred of the claims the treaty ceded up to the booking date.
- **Fallback `allocation_fallback`** (setting, default `aggregate`): line and origin cells in the treaty's scope (sections holding the movement's effective date, else all of the treaty; claims by the date of the attachment basis) by gross incurred at the booking date (claims) or gross written premium of the booking year (premium), labelled "aggregate allocation, an approximation"; `unallocated` leaves the amount on the treaty, labelled.
- **Cents.** Each movement's parts are rounded to cents and the remainder goes to the largest part, so a treaty's month adds up to its booked amount.
- **Currency.** Allocated amounts stay in the movement's currency (column `currency`); the views convert them per CV01 like any other amount. The application converts claims into the treaty currency at the occurrence date and premium at the booking date.
- **CV03 1.1.0 ceded fields.** Without ceded fields in claim_valuations, ceded paid and ceded reserve of a claim at a valuation date are the given and allocated claim movements booked up to it, with `ceded_source` (given, allocated, given and allocated).
- **Premium side.** Proportional sections cede their share of written premium in the inuring order (quota share on what surplus and facultative leave), commission = ceding_commission × ceded premium; a non-proportional section's deposit premium (100 %) is premium_rate × GNPI, GNPI being the written premium in scope after the proportional cessions; the stop loss gets the earned premium in scope after them. Written premium falls in a period by booking date (losses occurring) or policy start (risks attaching).
- **Events.** Refines record 0400: the per-claim approximation of an event cover applies when the claims carry no event code at all; when the data carry event codes, a claim without one is outside the event cover in every section (a contract year without events has nothing to cede, not an approximation).

## Comparison with the generator (information)
- **Tiny `reinsurance_heavy`** (fast test `tests/methods/test_mt21_ground_truth.py`): with `index_clause = occurrence_date`, the application on each claim's simulated gross ultimate reproduces `reinsurance_by_claim.csv`: quota share and surplus within 0.005 (the truth is rounded to cents), the four risk layers within 0.01, the event layer per treaty within 1 %. One truth claim is not reported at the cut-off and is not in the data.
- **Medium `reinsurance_heavy`** (ran once, generated into the scratchpad, 140 s to generate, 172 s in all): 11,281 truth claims, 11,225 reported. Risk layers: largest gap 0.004 (OD) and below 1e-8 (TPL L1 to L3, relative 3e-14); event layer: 2023 relative gap 1.3e-9. Quota share and surplus on PROPERTY: largest gaps 80 and 267 on 39 claims, all on policies in USD or EUR, 35 of them starting on a weekend: the generator converts a sum insured with its daily rate, the market feed publishes FX_SPOT on business days only, so the platform takes the last business day before (CV01). Not a defect of either side; the generator could publish its weekend rates.
- **The default clause (payment by payment) against the occurrence-date clause, ceded incurred at the cut-off on the data:** medium: XL_OD_RISK −9.2 %, XL_PROPERTY_CAT −8.0 %, XL_TPL_L1 −2.4 %, XL_TPL_L2 −14.3 %, XL_TPL_L3 −30.6 %; proportional treaties unchanged. Tiny (few claims): XL_OD_RISK −3.5 % (paid), XL_TPL_L1 −27 %, L2 +35 %, L3 −80 %. Expected: under payment by payment the terms stay at inception prices while late payments are deflated, so with the scenario's inflation less reaches the high layers.
- **Data against truth.** The run's data carry injected faults (extreme payments, a decreasing paid); the application on the data therefore differs from the reported ceded amounts on those claims, which is what the reconciliation of M08 is for. The allocation on medium: 59,475 movements, every one adds up (largest gap 9e-10), 19 s and 7.7 million parts (non-proportional premium spread over every policy earning in the month).

## Consequences
- The generator cedes quota-share premium on gross written premium while its claims cede on what the surplus leaves (QS_PROPERTY with SURPLUS_PROPERTY), and its excess-of-loss premium is a rate on gross written premium, not on GNPI: the computed premium of those treaties differs from the booked one by design. Listed for the generator's owner; the premium side follows the spec.
- The allocation of non-proportional premium to every earning policy is large on medium; M08 writes it aggregated to cells (record 0406).
