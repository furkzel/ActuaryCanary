# 0424 — The suggested causes of a material gap

## Context
CV12 asks that a material gap is listed with its likely causes. The causes are suggestions with their evidence, not findings of fact; a gap that no test supports says "no cause suggested". Each test is a small function of `ledger/causes.py` on plain data, so that it can be tested by hand (`tests/ledger/test_causes.py`) and each threshold has one place.

## Decisions (thresholds with their reasons)
- **Cut-off timing** (`CUT_OFF_TIMING`): the gap of the same entity and class in the month before or after is of the opposite sign and offsets this gap within 5 % of the larger of the two. A booking in the wrong month moves one amount from one month to the next, so the two gaps are equal and opposite; 5 % leaves room for the rate difference of record 0422, which moved the written pair of 840,195 by 0.04 %.
- **Currency** (`CURRENCY`): the month's platform figure contains rows in a foreign currency (their absolute amount, in the reporting currency, is the evidence) and the gap is at most 5 % of that amount. A rate difference within a month (the rate of the booking date against the monthly average or the month end) moves a foreign-currency amount by the movement of the rate in that month; for the lira 5 % is above a month's usual movement and well below a missing or doubled amount. The part is known for the flows (premium, commission, claims paid, expenses, RI); for UPR it is not computed, so no currency cause is suggested for it.
- **IBNR in the ledger outstanding** (`IBNR_IN_OUTSTANDING`): class OUTSTANDING only; in at least three months of the entity the gap is material and the ledger is above the case reserve, and the loading (ledger − platform) / platform stays within 10 % of its mean (spread over mean). A provision for IBNR is booked as a share of the reserve, so it shows as the same loading every month; one or two months, or a loading that wanders, are not enough to say so.
- **Unmapped account** (`UNMAPPED_ACCOUNT`): the accounts the profile does not map hold an absolute balance in the month (the closing balances of the entity) within 25 % of the absolute gap. The amount may sit on a suspense account; the tolerance allows for the partly offsetting items the books keep there. The sign of the gap is not asked: a suspense account holds amounts of either side.
- Every material row carries the codes joined by "; " (`suggested_causes`) and the evidence sentences joined by " | " (`cause_evidence`); a row with none says "no cause suggested". Rows that are not material carry neither. The page and the finding rules name them as "suggested".

## Consequences
- On the tiny reinsurance_heavy scenario without injected faults the written causes are all suggested (premium timing 4 of 4, IBNR 16 of 16); the written translation difference (2,432 on 1.9 million) is not material at 0.5 % and so has no row to explain; the seven RI gaps that the rate convention of the generator makes (record 0422) are suggested as currency; the suspense account is suggested nowhere because its balance (18,417) is far from every gap.
- A cause that two tests support (a timing gap in a foreign-currency month) lists both.
