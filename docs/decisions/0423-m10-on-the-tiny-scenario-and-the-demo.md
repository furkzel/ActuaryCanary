# 0423 — What M10 finds: the tiny scenario against the written differences, and the demo

## Context
`truth/ledger_gaps.csv` and `truth/ledger_differences.csv` (record 0244) say where the generator wrote a difference between the platform's figure and the ledger. M10 is scored against them on a generated tiny scenario, and run once on the demo scenario as information.

## The tiny scenario (reinsurance_heavy, tiny, no injected faults; `tests/ledger/test_truth.py`)
- **Material gaps.** Outside the class RI the material gaps are exactly the written ones: 20 entity-class-months, 4 of premium timing (two entities, the month booked in the next month, June and July 2023) and 16 of the IBNR loading in one entity's outstanding (the 8 % loading of the generator shows as a ledger 7.41 % above the platform, 8 % of the case reserve, month after month). The gaps are the written ones to the thousandth.
- **Written but not material.** The foreign-currency premium translated at the month end instead of the average rate (one month, 2,432 on 1.89 million, 0.13 %) is under the 0.5 % line and is flagged as not material, as the owner's threshold (record 0603) decides. The missing account row (account 690, class OTHER) is not in a reconciled class and so not a gap; the missing month shows in DV-LDG-07.
- **Not written, found.** Seven material RI gaps in one entity (largest 2,802 on a month of 258,581; 984 on a month of 16,376, 6 %). The truth has no RI gap. They come from the rate convention of the generator (a generator inconsistency carried from F2a): the generator converts premium flows at the average rate of the booking month, the platform (CV01, flows) at the rate of the booking date, and ceded premium is the large part of the small net figure of RI. Premium and commission differ by the same convention (up to 0.33 % and 0.49 % of the month) and stay under the line. Quota-share premium on gross and excess-of-loss premium on written premium (also F2a) are in the generator's figure and in the feed alike, so they do not appear as gaps; no weekend rate was needed. The generator is not changed.
- **Unmapped.** The unmapped account is the suspense account 297 of one entity (balance 18,417 at the end), nothing else; FN02 per entity and month equals the suspense balance over the absolute balances of the entity to 1e-12 (largest 0.07 %), zero for the other entities.
- **Causes against the written ones** (information): all 20 written causes are among the suggestions (premium timing 4 of 4, IBNR 16 of 16); no material gap is left with "no cause suggested"; the seven RI gaps are suggested as currency, which is what they are. Written translation (not material) and unmapped account (it moves no gap of a reconciled class: the 1.5 % went to the suspense account but the premium gap there is the timing one) have no row to explain.
- **With the default scenario** (injected faults left in) the platform's paid flows differ from the books' where an extreme payment or a decreasing paid_to_date was injected: the books hold the clean claim, the platform the feed. M10 shows those as CLAIMS_PAID gaps; they are the data faults of DV-CLV and not a mapping problem.

## The demo scenario (generated outside the repository, 15 s; `canary run` 27 s)
- 3 entities, 2,400 entity-class-months, **65 material gaps**: 48 OUTSTANDING (the IBNR loading, constant 7.41 %, largest 5.31 million), 9 CLAIMS_PAID (injected faults; the largest 1.13 million, 609 % of a month), 4 PREMIUM (timing, 3.38 million the largest, in one entity), 4 RI (the rate convention, up to 474).
- **Unmapped share**: the suspense account of one entity, 55,294 at the end, 79 months, the one finding of DV-LDG-09 (1 of 33 accounts); the share is under 0.1 %.
- **Causes suggested**: IBNR in the outstanding 48, cut-off timing 8 (the 4 premium gaps and 4 more that look like one), currency 4, no cause suggested 5 (claims paid with an injected fault: nothing offsets them, no foreign-currency part, no unmapped balance of their size).
- The profile was proposed (11 rules, "proposed, not confirmed") and saved; the run id, the manifest and every row say so.
- Nothing generated is committed.
