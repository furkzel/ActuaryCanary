# 0325 — The simulated range is scaled to the selected unpaid, and discounted for a line

## Context
RS05 says the distribution is "shifted multiplicatively to the selected mean" when the selection is not the chain-ladder reserve, and is "discounted per simulation". MT11 keeps the simulated reserve of every origin but the cash flows only by calendar period for the whole line, and the oldest origins can have no simulated unpaid at all while the selection has some.

## Decision
- Per origin: if the selected unpaid equals the chain-ladder reserve of the paid triangle (relative 1e-9) the simulations are used as they are, labelled "bootstrap". Otherwise each origin's simulations are multiplied by selected unpaid / mean of its simulations, so that the mean of the origin is the selected unpaid and its coefficient of variation is kept, labelled "bootstrap CV applied to the selected reserve". The line total is the sum of the scaled origins. An origin with a zero simulated reserve in every simulation stays zero (the spec: fully developed) and a note names it when its selected unpaid is not zero; the line's mean is then below the selected total by that amount.
- Discounted: MT11 has no cash flows by origin, so the discounted distribution exists for a line and for all lines, not for an origin (RS05 1.1.0 says so; the expected discounted unpaid of an origin is RS04). Per simulation, the share of the simulated cash flows that survives discounting is the discounted over the nominal simulated cash flows of that simulation, applied to the scaled line total: `discounted_b = total_b x share_b`. With a zero rate the two distributions are identical; the share is held inside the range of the factors and a simulation whose cash flows do not add to a positive amount takes the ratio of the means. MT11 pays its tail in one period, RS04 spreads it (0324): the two present values differ by that.
- The percentiles of the cash flows by period in `risk_cash_flows.csv` are those of MT11 multiplied by the ratio of the scaled to the unscaled mean of the line.
- TVaR: the mean of the simulated values at or above the empirical quantile of the tail level (0.99), the quantile by linear interpolation between order statistics; VaR the quantile at 0.995. Every row states the estimator, the number of simulations and the number of values in the tail, and the share of negative simulations. The closed form of the lognormal (RS03) is exact; the two differ by sampling error.

## Consequences
- RS05 1.1.0. The calibration on model triangles (behind ACTUARYCANARY_MONTE_CARLO=1, run once on 2026-11-07): the true reserve fell below the module's bootstrap 95th percentile in 0.953 of 150 triangles and below its VaR at 99.5 per cent in 0.993; the Mack lognormal 95th percentile covered 0.947 (information).
