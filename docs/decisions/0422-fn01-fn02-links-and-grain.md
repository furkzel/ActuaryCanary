# 0422 — FN01 and FN02: the links to the platform's measures and the grain

## Context
FN01 compares the platform's figure with the mapped ledger amount per entity, class and month; FN02 measures the share of the books the profile does not map (CV12, record 0421). The links from a class to a platform figure are written as data in one place so that each can be read and tested.

## Decisions
- **The table of links** is `LINKS` in `measures/ledger_platform.py`: class, the measure the figure comes from, flow or stock, the feeds it needs, the basis, and the function that builds it. PREMIUM is the written premium transactions of PR01 (the transactions of PR01 through the same function, so the same conversion); CLAIMS_PAID the paid flows of CL01 (differences of consecutive valuations, at the average rate of the month, CV01); OUTSTANDING the case reserve of CL02 at each month end; UPR PR03 at each month end; COMMISSION the premium rows of type COMMISSION; EXPENSE the expense rows of the feed; RI ceded premium less reinsurance commission less ceded paid on the CEDED basis (the rows RI01 and RI02 read). All GROSS except RI. INVESTMENT and OTHER are mapped, counted as mapped by FN02 and listed as "deliberately not reconciled", never as a gap.
- **Stocks at the period-end rate.** CL02's own view converts every valuation at the one rate of the cut-off (the development view); the ledger holds the outstanding at the month-end rate. FN01 therefore takes the case reserve at each month end in the original currency and converts it with CV01's stock rule (`Rule.STOCK`, the period-end rate of that month). PR03 already converts its stock at the period-end rate of the date it is asked for; it is asked at each month end of the premium file.
- **Grain.** Entity, class and month. The entity of a premium row is that of its policy (so `policies` is needed), of a claim that of its header in `claims`, of an expense row its own; rows without an entity (or an entity "UNKNOWN") are left out of the platform side and the notes say so. A class whose feed is not loaded, or whose entity cannot be told, is "not reconciled" with the reason. A segment or line split is not built: the request is refused with that reason; the profile's `line` is kept in the file.
- **Months.** A month is compared when the ledger has rows for the entity in it and the platform's feed covers it (from its first to its last month, a month inside without a row is zero). A month the ledger lacks is not computed (DV-LDG-10); a month outside the feed's range, and a year-to-date movement that cannot be told, are rows with no gap and a note.
- **Gap and materiality.** Amounts are rounded to cents; G = platform minus ledger; the share is G over the absolute ledger amount, not computed when the ledger amount is zero; the gap is material when |G| is greater than both `materiality_amount` and `materiality_ratio` times |L| (0 and 0.005 by default, `LedgerSettings`, run settings), strictly greater: a gap exactly on the line is not material.
- **IBNR in the ledger outstanding.** The ledger outstanding is compared with the case reserve alone; the comparison with case reserve plus the selected IBNR that CV12 allows "when the profile says so" is not built.
- **Rows in another currency** than the reporting currency are left out of the ledger side and counted in the notes; ledgers in several currencies are not built.

## Consequences
- On the no-fault tiny reinsurance_heavy scenario the platform's CLAIMS_PAID, OUTSTANDING, UPR and EXPENSE figures equal the generator's own platform amounts to the cent. PREMIUM, COMMISSION and RI differ from them by the rate convention only: the generator converts premium flows at the average rate of the booking month, the platform (CV01, flows) at the rate of the booking date. The largest premium difference is 0.33 % of the month, commission 0.49 %, both under the 0.5 % line; RI, a small net figure, reaches 6 % in one month and shows seven small gaps in one entity (see 0424 and the truth test).
