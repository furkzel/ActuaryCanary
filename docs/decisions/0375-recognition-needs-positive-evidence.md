# 0375 — Recognition takes a concept only on positive evidence

## Context
Records 0025–0029 and 0242: a workbook the platform does not know was read as a known concept (interest rates as market rows, which moved a run id), so the later feeds sit in `phase2/`. The rule was: the best concept is proposed if its required fields are all found or its score is at least 0.3. Measured on the sheets of the six feeds (clean and messy scenarios), the interest rates and the planned feeds (tiny files of `baseline`, `messy_headers` and `life_book`): the planned feeds were taken for `expenses`, `premiums` or `policies` (the reinsurance transactions with all six required fields of `premiums`).

## Decision
`judge` in `recognition/concepts.py` accepts the best concept only if all hold: (1) its score is above the next concept's by more than **0.30**; (2) at least **80 %** of its required fields are found; (3) either that margin is at least **0.50**, or at least **75 %** of the sheet's columns map to a field of the concept or are personal-data columns. Otherwise the sheet has no concept and `TableRecognition.rejection` holds the best guess and every reason ("best guess premiums score 0.95 (...), rejected: margin 0.30 over the next concept is not above 0.3; only 7 of 12 columns are explained ..."); the UI and the terminal list such a sheet as not recognised (a user can still choose its concept).
Why these values (measured): known sheets have margins 0.51–1.00, the interest rates 0.32 (clean) and 0.42 (messy), required share 0.83–1, explained share 0.88–1. The planned feeds have margins 0.00–0.35, required share 0.25–1 and explained share 0.27–0.71 (the 0.71 sheet, the recoveries, fails the 80 % rule on the messy files and the margin rule on the clean ones). Margin alone cannot separate the reinsurance transactions (0.30 clean, 0.35 messy) from the interest rates (0.32), the extra columns do; the clear margin of 0.50 keeps a known feed with many extra columns recognised.

## Consequences
- All sheets of the six feeds and of the interest rates are still recognised in `baseline`, `messy_headers` and `life_book`; all sheets of the planned feeds (treaties, transactions, recoveries, ledger, life in force, life events, fund flows) are now not recognised, so the run id of a folder that holds them no longer depends on a wrong reading. They stay in `phase2/`; the next item moves them out when it can recognise them.
- A sheet of an ambiguous concept (margin 0.31–0.49) with more than a quarter of unexplained columns is not recognised any more; the user can set its concept by hand.
- The pipeline is untouched. `ui/scan.py::reason_not_recognised` still says "below the threshold"; `TableRecognition.rejection` has the accurate text for the next item that touches the UI.
