# 0810 — Gate v1.3: the index-clause property, the Turkish label, the branch-name scrub

## Context
Two tests the earlier items left were red or intermittent, and the gate must not carry a defect it can explain. The number 0810 is the first of the gate's range (the highest earlier record is 0800).

## Decisions
- **The F2a property was wrong, the method is right.** Under the payment-by-payment index clause each payment is deflated, the layer is applied to the cumulative deflated payments and each ceded increment is inflated back at its own payment date. In nominal terms a higher retention can therefore cede more: the last increments of the layer fall on later, more inflated payments. The example `payments=[117, 4], retention=0, extra=1` (150 xs 1 cedes 169.46, 150 xs 0 cedes 169.36) is a correct result (it was found by Hypothesis in F2b, record 0420). `methods/reinsurance/` is not changed.
- **What the property now asserts.** Without an index and under `occurrence_date` the ceded total is monotone in the retention in nominal terms (paid and incurred). Under `payment_by_payment` the identities (net = gross - ceded, 0 <= ceded <= gross) hold on both bases, the deflated ceded paid total is monotone (increments divided by the price level of their valuation date against the inception), and the nominal ceded paid total of the higher retention exceeds that of the lower one by at most `extra` times the total movement of the price level over the claim's valuation dates (Abel summation: the cumulative difference of two layers shifted by `extra` is bounded by `extra`, and the deflated final difference is not positive). The deflated check is on the paid basis because the incurred reserve is valued at the index of the valuation date, not of a payment date (record 0401). The failing example is kept as an explicit `@example`. Checked with Hypothesis seeds 0 and 1 and without a fixed seed, after deleting the local `.hypothesis` folder: all pass.
- **The Turkish label is added, the test is not weakened.** `test_the_turkish_report_asks_only_for_texts_the_resource_has` named the parameter label "floor pseudo increments" (A1b, `floor_pseudo_increments`); `language.yaml` now has its Turkish text. The slow language tests all pass and nothing else is named.
- **Branch-name scrub.** `git grep` for tool-named branch prefixes in tracked files finds none (F2b removed the last ones); the only matches of the loose words are inside minified JavaScript of the committed reports and inside binary files, which are not branch names. Nothing was changed for it. The history is not touched; the owner words the merge commit.

## Consequences
The property keeps its strength where the method promises monotonicity and states the weaker, correct property where it does not. No number of any run changes.
