# 0400 — Treaty application: sections, stages, scope, what is left out and the labels

## Context
F2a builds the application step of MT21: the programme of `reinsurance_treaties` applied to gross claim amounts, giving ceded and net per claim, treaty and valuation (`methods/reinsurance/`). CV09 and the treaties spec fix the scope rules and the inuring order (record 0604) but leave several choices open.

## Decisions
- **Section.** The unit of terms is a section: the rows of one `treaty_id` and `inception_date` whose terms agree (the feed has one row per line with the same terms). Rows of a period that disagree on terms give one section per set of terms. Aggregate terms, event grouping and stop loss work per section, so across the lines of a treaty and contract period.
- **Stages.** Facultative, surplus, quota share, risk excess of loss, event excess of loss, then the aggregate stage (stop loss, and an excess of loss with `loss_basis` AGGREGATE). Each stage works on what the stages before it leave. The sections of one stage work side by side on the same amounts: two quota shares on one line cede their shares of the same amount (as the generator adds cessions), and the layers of a programme (L1, L2, L3) apply to the same retained amount. When the sections of a stage together cede more than the amount they work on (overlapping layers, shares above one), each is scaled down in proportion, so ceded never exceeds gross.
- **Scope (CV09).** Entity, segment and line (empty means all); LOSSES_OCCURRING by `claims.occurrence_date`, RISKS_ATTACHING by the policy's `start_date`, both within [inception, expiry). A claim that matches a section's attributes but lacks the date its basis needs is out of scope and listed (`out_of_scope`, one row per treaty, claim and reason).
- **Left out and named.** A row without the terms of its type (DV-RTY-05), with a missing required field (DV-RTY-01), an unknown type (DV-RTY-02), expiry not after inception (DV-RTY-03), a share outside 0 to 1 (DV-RTY-06) or a negative term (DV-RTY-07) is left out with its reason; the quality rules still list the same rows.
- **Proportional.** Quota share cedes `cession_share` of what is left; surplus cedes min(max(SI − r, 0), n·r)/SI with SI the policy's `sum_insured` converted into the treaty currency at the policy start, a claim without a usable sum insured (no policy, empty or zero) is not ceded and counted in the section's notes; facultative cedes the share of the claim's policy given by the policy-level movements (the input assembly of stage 3 derives it), nothing without them. The placed share multiplies every section.
- **Excess of loss and stop loss.** Applied to each unit cumulatively at every valuation date held, on paid and on incurred: per claim (RISK), per event (claims with the same `event_code`), or all claims of the section (stop loss, aggregate excess of loss). A unit of several claims shares its ceded amount in proportion to the claims' amounts at the date; ceded reserve is kept between zero and the claim's reserve (the generator's rule for events). A closed claim keeps the ceded amounts of its last valuation row.
- **Events.** When no claim in a section's scope has an event code, the event cover is applied per claim and labelled "event cover applied per claim, an approximation" (MT21 failure mode). When some have codes, claims without one are not covered by the event treaty (a catastrophe cover covers events; the generator does the same) and their number is in the notes.
- **Stop loss.** C = EP·min(L, max(0, X/EP − R)), a layer with retention R·EP and limit L·EP on the section's total. EP is the earned premium of the section's scope retained after the inuring proportional treaties, given to the method (the premium side builds it); without it the stop loss is not applied and the note says so.
- **Currency.** Claim amounts in another currency than the treaty's are converted into the treaty currency at the rate of the occurrence date for the layer and converted back at the same rate (the rate of a claim does not move, so a risk layer is the same as converting its terms); a claim without a rate is listed and left out of that section.
- **Labels and notes** sit on each section result (`notes`) and reach the layer table of M08: the index clause in use, a missing index series, events applied per claim, claims without event codes, claims without a usable sum insured or exchange rate, a stop loss without premium, facultative cover without policy-level cessions.

## Consequences
- The hand fixture MT21 gains event excess of loss (150 xs 120 on two events), stop loss (30 % xs 60 % loss ratio on a given premium) and the five-stage programme; its manifest is rewritten by the builder and so carries `checked_by_owner: false` until the owner looks at the new cases.
- Property tests: net = gross − ceded exactly, ceded within [0, gross], a higher retention never makes the programme cede more, an empty programme leaves net equal to gross, quota share is linear.
