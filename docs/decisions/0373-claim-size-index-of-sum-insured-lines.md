# 0373 — Claim size of a sum-insured line is indexed by the calendar day, declared per line

## Context
Record 0368: GROUP_TERM_LIFE claims were `share × sum insured at the prices of the policy start`, never indexed after. The truth said claims inflation 0, but claim size grew 34.8 % a year by origin year (high_inflation), and MT15 reported 32.8 % a year. PROPERTY was checked as well (`high_inflation medium`, regular non-nil claims, 2016–2025): its mean cost at base-date prices has no trend (33,747 in 2016, 32,709 in 2025; the range 29,000–62,000 is sampling), the cap at the sum insured binds in at most 2.5 % of claims in a year, and its claims are already indexed by the payment day. PROPERTY needs no change; the origin-level index was only on the fixed-share body.

## Decision
- A new key on a line's severity, `sum_insured_index: {by: occurrence | settlement, prices: true | false}`, allowed on `fixed_share_of_sum_insured` bodies only. The claim is `share × sum insured at base-date prices` (the sum at policy start divided by the price factor of that day), moved by the line's claims index (`prices` × `(1 + superimposed_inflation)^years`) on the day of the loss (`occurrence`) or of each payment (`settlement`; a line that pays once pays at settlement). `prices: false` with `superimposed_inflation: 0` is a declared index of 0: claim size has no trend by origin or by calendar year.
- Without the key the old behaviour stays available and is named: the claim is tied to the prices of the policy start.
- The baseline declares `{by: occurrence, prices: true}` for GROUP_TERM_LIFE (an indexed sum insured); every scenario extends the baseline, so every scenario changes on that line only. Premium stays on the sum insured at policy start.
- True index per line: `truth/inflation.csv` column `claims_index_<line>` is the index on the day named in the new `truth/claim_size_index.csv` (columns line, sum_insured_line, index_day = payment | occurrence | policy_start, follows_prices, superimposed_inflation, claims_index_column). Values on the baseline: MOTOR_OD, MOTOR_TPL, PROPERTY `payment` (CPI × superimposed 3 %, 5 %, 2 %); GROUP_TERM_LIFE `occurrence` (CPI, superimposed 0); `claims_index_GROUP_TERM_LIFE` changes from flat 1 to CPI / 100. For `policy_start` lines the column stays flat 1 and the trend of claim size by origin is the price factor at the start.
- Tests built from hand-checkable numbers (`tests/synthetic/test_sum_insured_index.py`): paid amount = sum at base prices × 1 (declared 0), × price factor of the loss day (occurrence), × price factor of the payment day (settlement), × price factor of the start (no key).
- Premises rewritten: `test_truth.py` expected `claims_index_GROUP_TERM_LIFE` flat 1 (now CPI/100); `test_phase2_reinsurance.py::test_the_heavy_programme_is_hit_in_every_layer` compared the ceded ultimate with the gross of every line and passed by a margin of 21 % against 20 %; with group life claims at loss-day prices the base grew by 21 %, so the base is now the non-life segment the programme covers (ceded is 56 % of it).

## Consequences
- Group life ultimates rise by about 21 % on `reinsurance_heavy tiny` (79.9 M to 96.6 M): the old claims lagged prices by up to a policy year. MT15 on GROUP_TERM_LIFE now has a calendar index of CPI to recover; its criterion needs no change (not run here). The limitation note of MT15 1.0.1 (0368) stays true for a scenario without the key; it describes the baseline no longer. Spec not edited (owner).
- Committed fixtures under `tests/fixtures/synthetic/` and `examples/` are older than the generator until the gate refreshes them.
