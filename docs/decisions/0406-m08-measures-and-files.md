# 0406 — M08: the new measures, files and wiring

## Context
F2a stage 4 wires M08 Reinsurance into a run as M07 and M09 were (records 0386 to 0388).

## Decisions
- **Measures.** RI03 (ceded share by treaty, CEDED basis: reported ceded incurred and ceded premium of each treaty and treaty year over the gross incurred at the cut-off and the gross written premium in its scope; notes for no bookings, gross zero, aggregate allocation and a quota share more than 0.5 points away from its cession; built per treaty and treaty year only), RI04 (reinsurance result ratio per origin year: ceded incurred of claim_valuations, commission earned, ceded premium earned, reinstatement premium allocated to the origin year, over gross earned premium; always the incurred view, labelled, as no ceded selection is built; an origin year outside every treaty period is not computed), RI05 (sample standard deviation over origin years of the gross ultimate loss ratio of the selected ultimates (MT10) and of the net one, net = gross ultimate × net/gross incurred of the programme's application per origin, as MT21 assumes; at least five origin years; incurred view without a selection). They are in `measures/reinsurance_programme.py`; RI01 and RI02 are unchanged. Default views: RI03 by treaty, RI04 by origin, RI05 by segment and line.
- **The run of M08** (`modules/reinsurance.py`, `ReinsuranceRun`) is built once per run in the cache of the measure inputs, from the feeds up to the cut-off, with the run setting `reinsurance`; measures and files read the same object.
- **Files** (written only when M08 ran, through `write_table`, listed in the manifest after `recovery_reconciliation.csv`): `ceded_by_treaty.csv` (one row per treaty and treaty year: terms, the index clause in use, claims and gross in scope, computed and reported ceded paid and incurred, computed and reported ceded premium, commission and reinstatement premium, layer use, notes), `ceded_allocation.csv` (the movements as given or allocated by treaty, type, method, target, line, origin or policy-start year and currency, with the number of parts; aggregated so that non-proportional premium spread over every earning policy stays small), `reinsurance_reconciliation.csv` (record 0405), `ceded_by_size_band.csv` (computed ceded share of gross incurred at the cut-off per claim size band, reporting currency, and treaty). `programme_comparison.csv` follows with the what-if (stage 5).
- **Wiring.** M08, MT21, CV09, RI03 to RI05 are `implemented: true` (still draft); MT21 joins the methods the runner counts as built; the module's status comes from the quality report (`required_all: reinsurance_treaties`), so a folder without treaties skips M08 with its skip reason and writes none of its files.
- **CV03 1.1.0 in a run.** When claim_valuations carries no ceded field and the transactions feed is loaded, the given and allocated claim movements become the ceded fields before any measure runs (record 0403); with the fields present nothing changes.

## Consequences
- Tests: `tests/measures/test_ri03_*`, `test_ri04_*`, `test_ri05_*` (hand books), `tests/modules/test_m08_reinsurance.py` (treaty table, a built difference, allocation table, size bands, settings, the skip), `tests/modules/test_m08_pipeline_slow.py` (slow, written and not run: a run of the tiny reinsurance_heavy). `tests/test_pipeline.py` now lists M08 as skipped on the tiny baseline (premise changed by this item).
