# 0752 — A1a: one selected unpaid of all lines, the bridge to the simulated mean, one definition per table

## Context
R07: KF33 called 568.74 m (the mean of the bootstrap) "the selected unpaid of all lines", KF34 called 569.18 m (the sum of the selected unpaid by origin) the same. R05: the two differ by 441,978 TRY because an origin that is fully developed in the simulated triangle carries no simulated development (records 0325, 0349). R06: the table of the discounted basis put the simulated discounted mean (RS05) beside the discount effect of the expected cash flows (RS04); its rows did not add up (on the demo 314.82 m + 249.64 m is not 568.74 m).

## Decisions
- **One name, one figure.** "The selected unpaid" is the selected ultimate less the paid (RS02 with MT10), added over the lines: 569,183,675 on the demo. It is the first column of the nominal table and the first step of the bridge, and the figure of KF34. "The mean of the range" (nominal block) and "the mean of the simulation" are the mean of the bootstrap where it ran, else of Mack: 568,741,697. KF33 now speaks of the mean of the range and gives the selected unpaid in a new fact `range_selected` (the sum of `selected_total` of the lines), with the pointer to the steps; KF34 says that its figure is the selected unpaid of the uncertainty page and not the mean of the simulation. Conditions are unchanged. RP01 1.3.2.
- **The bridge (R05)**, steps as rows, the lines and all lines as columns: selected unpaid; less the origins without simulated development; plus the difference of the simulation for the other origins; mean of the simulation, nominal; on the discounted basis also less the discount effect on the simulated mean (RS05) and the mean of the simulation, present value. Below it a list of the origins left out with their selected unpaid (demo: NON_LIFE · MOTOR_TPL, origin 2016, 441,978; that is the whole difference). An origin is left out when its selected unpaid is half a cent or more and its simulated mean is below half a cent. The "difference for the other origins" is the remainder (simulated mean less selected unpaid of the other origins): zero for a line whose simulation M12 scales to the selected unpaid, a sampling difference for a line whose selected unpaid is the chain ladder's own. Rows add up before rounding; the footnote says so. The all-lines column is the sum of the line columns and exists only when every line has a simulation; what is simulated is not changed and M12's code is not touched (the amounts are read from `LineRisk`).
- **Present values (R06).** Two tables, one definition each. (1) The range table: on the discounted basis it shows, per line, the nominal mean of the range, the discount effect on that mean and the mean of the range in present value (nominal less effect is the mean; effect and means come from the same row of `risk_measures.csv`, RS05 for lines with a bootstrap, RS04 where only that exists). (2) "Present value of the selected unpaid (expected cash flows)": selected unpaid, discount effect (RS04), present value (RS04); selected less effect is the present value. The footnote says why the two discount effects differ (249.64 m of the cash flows against 253.92 m of the simulation on the demo). The summary table of the reserving page already holds RS04 only and is unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** every row of `risk_measures.csv`, the simulation, the scaling (0325, 0349).

The bridge was also compared with `risk_measures.csv` of the demo run: left out 441,977.85, nothing else.
