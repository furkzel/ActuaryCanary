# 0284 — Details of MT15, MT16, MT22 and AE02 that the specs leave open

## Decision
- MT15: the trend is fitted on min(trend_periods, I) diagonals (the fixture has four, the default is five). An assumed rate is annual and is turned into a rate per development period. Residuals are X / (n r lambda) - 1. A calendar index that is not positive on the trend diagonals stops the method with a reason.
- MT16: catastrophe claims come as their own cumulative triangle; their ultimate is its chain ladder (at least one origin), otherwise the latest value. No mature origin at all stops the method; one or two give the constant-severity WARNING of the spec. The frequency trend needs two origins with positive exposure.
- MT22: the past cut-off removes the latest h / development-period diagonals by calendar index and drops the empty origins and columns. This is the rule of `modules.reserving.truncate_triangle`, and a test checks that both give the same cells. The default runners are MT02 and, given premium, MT03, MT04, MT05 and MT10 with their defaults (the Cape Cod ratio of the same as-of triangle is the data-derived prior, record 0011). The premium is today's earned premium of the origins; for accident-year origins at year-end cut-offs it equals the premium known at the past cut-off. Past ultimates computed elsewhere (`modules.reserving.earlier_result`) can be passed in instead. AE01 beside the errors uses the MT02 pattern of the past cut-off. The range calibration counts the origins with a reserve range (MT06; MT11 when a stream is given) whose reference reserve lies inside the 5 to 95 per cent range.
- AE02 is computed by `methods.back_testing` (`ae02_errors`, `weighted_errors`); the measure engine wires it with the integration. Its test is at the path the spec names.
