# 0625 — MT18 scans the candidate thresholds with 200 bootstrap samples (MT18 1.1.0)

## Context
Record 0623 left the medium budget test failing (`test_medium_runs_inside_its_budget_cold_and_warm`, 45 s cold and 30 s warm) because MT18 refitted 500 bootstrap samples at each of 12 candidate thresholds, 16 to 35 s on the medium baseline of this machine, and offered four ways out. The owner chose: the goodness-of-fit bootstrap of the threshold scan (ForwardStop) defaults to 200 samples; budgets stay as they are, and a slower machine is not a reason to raise them.

## What the profile showed (2026-10-26, medium baseline, four groups)
- MT18 alone took 26 to 30 s here (cProfile 30.6 s). 36 candidate fits were bootstrapped over the four groups (a candidate with fewer than 50 excesses is not tried); 29.8 s of the 30.6 s are in `gpd.bootstrap_p_value`, and 24.8 s of those in one function, `gpd._profile`, the profile log-likelihood of all bootstrap samples at one value of k (a `log1p` over a 500 × n matrix; n up to 1,135 excesses). The refits themselves (grid of 37 points and 44 golden-section steps per sample) are the cost: 128 evaluations of `_profile` per candidate. The Anderson–Darling statistics (2.3 s), the simulation of the samples (0.6 s) and everything else (single fits, standard errors, profile interval, mean excess, yearly tables) are about 1 s together.
- MT17 took 6.7 s; it shares `gpd.fit_matrix` for its Pareto II family.

## Decision
- **Exact speed-up first (changes no number).** `fit_matrix` evaluated the profile twice per golden-section step although only one point of each row is new; it now evaluates it once. `_profile` takes the logarithm in place instead of allocating a second matrix. Both are the same floating-point operations on the same values, so the results are equal to the last bit: every cell of all ten MT18 tables and all nine MT17 tables of the medium baseline was compared before and after (repr of every value, 19 tables, 0 differences). Evaluations per candidate fall from 128 to 84. Measured here (not idle, two runs each, seconds for MT18 on the medium baseline): 500 samples 27.5 and 29.4 before, 17.0 and 19.6 after; 200 samples 11.6 and 11.7 before, 7.4 and 8.1 after.
- **Default change.** `gof_bootstrap` of MT18 is 200 (it was 500): MT18 specification 1.1.0, `evt.GOF_BOOTSTRAP`, and so the settings record (`severity.MT18.gof_bootstrap`) and the run id. MT17's default was already 200 and does not change. The user may still ask for more samples through the option.
- **Final fit.** The owner's wording keeps the old number of samples "for the final fit at the selected threshold". The specification does not separate two uses: its one parameter says "at each candidate", and the code has one use, the scan. The fit at the selected threshold (xi, sigma, standard errors, profile-likelihood interval, tail quantiles, return levels, expected shortfall) has no bootstrap of its own; the p-value shown for the selected threshold in `extreme_candidates.csv` is the scan's. There is therefore no second number to keep at 500 and none was added: a new bootstrap at the selected threshold would be new behaviour, a new output or a changed one, beyond this item. Its p-value now has the resolution 1/201 instead of 1/501. If the owner wants the selected threshold re-tested with 500 samples, it is a further parameter and a further amendment (cost: one more bootstrap per group, about one ninth of the old scan).
- Tolerances are not touched. The statements that name the scan size are the parameter of the specification, the constant and the settings test; no benchmark or test statement names 500 for MT18. The Monte Carlo tests already use 100 bootstrap samples (record 0303) and keep their meaning.

## Consequences
- Run ids of the golden copy and of the three examples change (`HANDOVER.md` lists every change and its cause). On the two tiny examples MT18 fits no group, so only the run id, the setting and the SHA-256 of the report move. On the demo only the columns `p_value` and `forward_stop_mean` of `extreme_candidates.csv` move (by up to 0.07 in a p-value); the selected thresholds, the generalised Pareto fits, the tail quantiles, the return levels and the threshold comparison are equal to the last digit, because ForwardStop chose the same threshold in every group. On other data the choice can differ: the p-values are Monte Carlo estimates, now from 200 samples.
- The budgets of the medium test are not changed.

## Result on this machine (not idle; 12th Gen Intel i5-12500H laptop, browser and other programs open)
- The severity step of the medium baseline falls from 22.9–33.6 s (0623) to 13.0–13.1 s (`just bench`, cold and warm).
- The budget test still fails cold: 58.3, 54.6 and 57.9 s against 45 s in three runs of the test alone (a fourth figure, in the full `just test-all` run, was 59.8 s). `just bench` shows 58.0 s cold and 44.7 s warm (without the import) against 45 and 30; without the severity step the run would still take about 45 s cold and 32 s warm here. The other steps are the cost on this machine (reading the workbooks 13.8 s cold, measures and modules 10.0 s, writing the tables 9.5 s). What is left to decide is the budget or the machine, not MT18; the budgets were not touched.
