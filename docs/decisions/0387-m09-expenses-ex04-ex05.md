# 0387 — M09 expenses with EX04 and EX05: what the specifications leave open

## Context
Stage 7 of F1bc builds the measures EX04 (administration cost per policy) and EX05 (claims-handling cost per claim) and the module M09 (all 0.1.0, draft, text unchanged, `implemented: true`). EX01 to EX03 exist since version 1.0 and are not changed; M05 is not changed.

## Decisions
- **Per calendar period only.** Both unit costs are defined for a calendar period (their grain); a request without the `calendar_period` breakdown is refused with that reason.
- **EX04.** Numerator: expenses of type ADMINISTRATION of the period, converted like EX02 (CV01). Denominator: the policy days of cover in the period over its days; cover is `[start_date, end_date)` and ends at the cancellation date when there is one; a policy id is counted once. The in-force count is integrated over its changes, so the cost does not grow with policies times periods.
- **EX05.** Numerator as EX03: the flows of `expense_allocated_to_date` plus the expenses of type CLAIMS_HANDLING_UNALLOCATED of the period; with one component only the measure says which (`components`: "allocated only", "unallocated only"). Denominator: the denominator of CL09 (claims open at the start of the period plus claims reported in it), taken from the CL09 function itself; in the first period of the file the open count at its start is unknown and the value is not computed, as in CL09.
- **A month without expense rows** (of the type the measure reads, at the level asked) is a missing booking: a period that holds such a month inside the span of the expense file is not computed, and the note names the months ("no administration expense rows in 2024-05: not computed"). It is never read as zero. For a period longer than a month this is strict on purpose: a yearly cost with a month missing would be too low.
- **Value-date prices (CV02).** On the inflation-adjusted basis the expense rows are restated with the price index of their month to the value date of the run (`restatement_factors`, the function behind the inflation-adjusted triangles); the allocated flows of EX05 are restated by `convert.bases` as in the triangles. The basis follows the setting of the run, like every measure whose specification allows it; the notes name the price date and the index. A row without an index value is left out and counted. The ORIGINAL currency view has no value-date prices (the index belongs to the reporting currency) and refuses expenses in several currencies instead of adding them.
- **Unallocated expenses are never spread.** A view by segment or line holds only the expenses booked with that level (the rule of EX02, record 0051); the rest is counted in a note. The table of the module (`modules/expenses.py`, the output `expenses_by_period.csv`) lists every amount by month, type and the level it was booked at (entity, segment, line; empty means not allocated), nominal and at value-date prices; the column at value-date prices is empty with the reason when there is no price index. The module specification wins over any older wording about allocating expenses.
- **Views of the module.** M09 asks the same measures as M05 at more depth: EX01 and EX02 by period, by month, and by segment, line and month; EX03 by period and by month; EX04 and EX05 by period and by segment, line and period (`MODULE_VIEWS` in `modules/views.py`; a view may name its own period). M05 keeps the default views, so its results are unchanged, and the yearly view both modules ask is computed once. The headline of the page (last twelve months against the twelve before) is read from the monthly views.
- **Trends against premium and claims** are the ratios themselves over time (EX01 and EX02 against earned premium, EX03 against paid claims, by month); the specification states no further trend measure and none is added.

## Consequences
- Tests: `tests/measures/test_ex04_administration_cost_per_policy.py`, `tests/measures/test_ex05_claims_handling_cost_per_claim.py` (hand-computed, the edge cases of the specs, value-date prices), `tests/modules/test_m09_expenses.py` (expenses alone, every feed, the skip reason, the views of M05 unchanged, the table at the level booked). Written, not run: `tests/modules/test_m09_medium_slow.py`.
- On the tiny baseline of the generator: administration cost per policy 2,990 (2022) to 10,743 (2025) in nominal TRY; the claims-handling cost per claim of 2025 is not computed because June 2025 has no unallocated claims-handling row.
