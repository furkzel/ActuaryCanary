# 0390 — Mapping, status marks and faults of the four feeds (stage 5 of F1bc)

## Context
Stage 5 wires the four feeds into a run and tests them on the generated scenarios. Records 0385 to 0389 are kept for the golden and the modules, so the choices of this stage are here.

## Decisions
- **A run reads them.** `CHECKED_CONCEPTS` in `pipeline.py` gains the four concepts: their sheets are mapped, typed, checked, listed among the inputs and their spec versions recorded; nothing computes on treaties, transactions and the ledger yet (M08, M10).
- **Mapping profiles and the confirmation** needed no change: they work per concept from the dictionary. `ui/scan.py::reason_not_recognised` now returns `TableRecognition.rejection` (the best guess and every reason, record 0375) instead of "is below the threshold".
- **The header `CERDIT`.** The messy ledger writes its credit column with two letters swapped. The fuzzy layer needs a ratio of 85; one slip in a six-letter word gives 83.3, so the column is not mapped and waits for the user. No constant was changed and no alias added for it: lowering the threshold for short words would touch the six feeds, and an alias for one generated slip would be tuning to the generator. With `--yes` the messy ledger is read without `credit`, and DV-LDG-06 is then not evaluated for it. The test `test_a_ledger_mapping_is_edited_saved_and_used_again` maps the column by hand, saves the profile, reads the folder again with it and gets the canonical data of the generator. Every other column of the four messy feeds maps to the clean canonical data without help.
- **Status marks.** M07 and M09 take their mark from `required_all` and `required_any` of their specifications through the existing rule of the quality engine (M07 able on `claim_valuations` alone, M09 on `expenses` alone, unable with its `skip_reason` otherwise). Nothing is added for M08 and M10.
- **Faults.** `truth/injected_faults.csv` holds no fault of the four feeds. What the generator wrote into them is found with the count of its own checker (record 0384): the missing ledger row (DV-LDG-07), the books that do not balance (DV-LDG-08), the reversed ceded movements (DV-RTR-10), and in `reinsurance_heavy` the ceded amount above gross (DV-RTR-08) and the disagreeing ceded fields (DV-RTR-09). The recoveries agree with `recoveries_truth.csv` and with the to-date fields (DV-RCV-08 lists nothing). The kinds `premium_timing`, `ibnr_in_outstanding` and `translation` of `ledger_differences.csv` and the file `ledger_gaps.csv` are differences between the ledger and the platform's measures: they belong to FN01, FN02 and M10 (the ledger item), and `unmapped_account` to DV-LDG-09, which waits for the mapping profile of CV12. No rule built here is meant to find them.
- **DV-RTR-11 on the messy files.** The generator's checker reports 26 differing totals on `messy_headers`; a run reports none, like on `life_book` from which the scenario starts. The checker reads the raw messy premiums, where a type may be stored with the opposite sign; the canonical step flips such a type (DV-PRM-08) before the comparison.

## Consequences
- Tests: `tests/quality/test_scenario_faults.py`, `tests/quality/test_module_status_m07_m09.py`, `tests/cli/test_confirm_new_feeds.py`, the profile test in `tests/recognition/test_new_feeds.py`; `scenario_checked` in `tests/conftest.py` maps, types and checks a generated scenario once per worker.
- `tests/recognition/test_messy.py` (committed fixtures) expects no sheet of the four feeds in the fixture folders.
