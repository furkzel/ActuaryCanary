# 0620 — The tier B reserving methods run in M04, beside tier A

## Context
P24 built MT11 to MT16, MT22 and AE02 in `methods/` and left them to the integration (0280, 0281). The handover named what the integration must do. This record says where each method runs, on which inputs, and what it writes.

## Decision
- `modules.reserving.run_reserving` runs the tier B methods for every group of the run's own basis (reinsurance basis, currency view, value basis of the triangles) when `MethodInputs.tier_b` is given; the pipeline always gives it. Without it nothing of tier B runs (the tests of tier A call `run_reserving` that way). They run only in the full pass, not in `earlier_result`.
- Where: MT11 and MT15 on the paid triangle, MT12 on paid and incurred together, MT13 on the triangle of its parameter `measure` (incurred) with the MT03 result as prior (MT04 if the parameter says so), MT14 on the chain ladder of both triangles, MT16 on counts and amounts built for it, MT22 on the incurred triangle against the MT10 selection. Every parameter comes from the register (`judgement_tier_b.parameters`, scope `SEGMENT/LINE/<measure>`), and the entry in force of each goes to `assumptions.csv`, the manifest and the report appendix.
- MT14 goes through `judgement_tier_b.chain_ladder_with_tail` wherever MT02 runs. The default tail stays 1.0, so every tier A number is the same as before (checked: `triangles.csv`, `factors.csv`, `chain_ladder.csv`, `ultimates.csv`, `reserve_range.csv`, `cash_flows.csv`, `measures.csv` and `lineage*.csv` of the three examples are byte for byte equal). A fitted tail replaces the MT02 tail only through the register entry `MT14 selected_curve`.
- MT11 takes the MT02 tail of the paid chain ladder unless the register gives its own `tail_factor` (the spec: "default: MT02 tail_factor"); the entry in force then says the tail used.
- MT15 and MT16 need inputs that the run's triangles do not hold: the non-nil reported counts and the amounts of the claims without catastrophe claims, the catastrophe claims apart (CV08: an event code), severity at value-date prices when the price index is loaded (otherwise nominal, with a diagnostic), and the earned exposure of each origin (CL05). `modules.tier_b_inputs.TierBSource` builds them from the same feeds, rates and bases with `build_triangles`, each set on first use. MT15 takes the ultimate counts of MT16 and the nominal paid amounts of the claims without catastrophe claims (the choice of the ground-truth test of P24).
- MT22 re-runs MT02 and, with premium, MT03, MT04, MT05 and MT10 with their defaults at the past cut-offs (`premium_runners`); its horizons are 12, 24 and 36 months, and the MT11 re-run for the range calibration draws from the group's own stream. AE02 is the error table of that back-test: the measure engine hands it on through the port (`METHOD_OUTPUTS`, `ReservingPort.measure("AE02")`). No module lists AE02 yet (M12 and M13 will), so no module computes it; `back_test.csv` holds the same rows.
- A method that refuses to run keeps its reason: one line per method and reason in the notes of M04 ("MT11 bootstrap did not run for 3 of 4 groups: ..."), which the report lists under "What did not run and why", and one row with the reason in its output file. A refusal makes M04 limited, as the refusal of a tier A method does. No refusal is replaced by a figure.
- Seeds and defaults: `registry.settings(RUN_SEED)` is part of the settings record (`tier_b`), so a change of seed or of any default is another run id; the `registry.randomness` rows are written for the methods that ran. Each group draws from `seeding.generator(seed, *group)`; the MT22 re-run from `generator(seed, *group, "MT22")`.
- Outputs, all unrounded (CV06): `tier_b_ultimates.csv` (MT12, MT13, MT15, MT16 per origin, or the reason), `tier_b_parameters.csv` (fitted quantities, long), `bootstrap_summary.csv`, `bootstrap_cash_flows.csv`, `bootstrap_residuals.csv`, `bootstrap_distribution_<segment>_<line>.parquet` (one per group where MT11 ran), `back_test.csv` (MT22 and AE02). The diagnostics of every method are in `diagnostics.csv`; the thin-evidence line names the tier B methods that ran.
- `implemented: true` for MT11 to MT18, MT22 and AE02. MT19 stays `implemented: false`.
- A defect of P24 came out and is fixed in the glue, not in a method: `chain_ladder_with_tail` passed `tail_factor` twice to the MT02 call when the register selected a curve and the module also gave the run's tail (TypeError). The tail setting of the run now gives way to the fitted one in that call; the P24 tests did not pass a tail option and so did not see it. A test of the wiring covers it.

## Consequences
- The run ids, the golden copy, the three examples and the guide moved (listed in the handover); no number of tier A moved.
- The module specs M03, M04 and M06 are not amended (0622); the method notes of the tier B methods and the report pages are item P27.
- The ultimates of MT16 are at value-date prices (labelled in `basis` and `note`); they are not comparable with the nominal ultimates of the other methods in the same file.
