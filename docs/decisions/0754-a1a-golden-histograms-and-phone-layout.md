# 0754 — A1a: histogram axis, phone layout and the golden copies

## Context
R21: about 96 % of the simulated mass sat in two bars of each histogram because of a few extreme draws, and a phone showed a page of 1,064 px.

## Decisions
- **Histogram axis (R21).** The bars and the axis run from the 0.5th to the 99.5th percentile of the simulations of the figure (40 bars as before); the caption under each histogram states the axis, the minimum and the maximum of all simulations, the share of simulations outside the axis and the marks (mean, VaR, TVaR) that fall beyond it. A mark outside the axis is not drawn and is listed in the caption. `risk_distribution.parquet` and the bootstrap parquet files are untouched, so the data stay complete. On the demo the histogram of NON_LIFE · MOTOR_TPL now shows its shape (looked at in a screenshot of the demo report).
- **Phone width (R21).** Looked at with Chromium and Playwright (mobile emulation, outside the repository) on a fresh demo report: the page fitted at 390 px (`scrollWidth` 390) but not at 320 px (336, from the minimum of 320 px of the chart grid). Changes: the chart grid uses `minmax(min(320px, 100%), 1fr)`, charts and scroll boxes are limited to the width of their box, and below 640 px (screen only) the margins, the cover list, the navigation columns, the tiles and the cell padding are tighter. At 390 and 320 px the page does not scroll sideways, with every `details` open and on both value bases; the review's 1,064 px was not reproduced on this build. Every table of the page sits in a `.scroll` box (a test). The `max-width: 1080px` rule and the print rules are unchanged; the new rules are `@media screen`. The print stylesheet was checked by reading the CSS only: UNVERIFIED by eye.
- **Golden.** `just golden` ran once (2026-11-12); the diff was read key by key in both copies. Changed entries and their causes (the same in both languages): `run_id` English `e714ce9617e26f80` → `5daa3cb3d3c5cebd`, Turkish `d42fe1b25de13deb` → `ccc81ac39b2fb50d` (cause: RP01 1.3.0 → 1.3.2 is part of the run id; the report text is not); `findings_spec` and the specs entry RP01 1.3.0 → 1.3.2; the sentence of KF04 for LIFE (the clause that says what is compared); the footnotes `headline` and `histogram` and the new `bridge` and `present_values` footnotes of the uncertainty page; new keys `bridge` (null) and `present_values` (empty) of the tiny run, which has no bootstrap and no curve; `no_fit` (false) of the four severity blocks; `range_coverage` ("90% range; no back-test") of the four lines; the new key `module_runs` (the state of each module in the run); the note of the quality tile (failed rules and the sentence on what the score measures). No estimate, factor, reserve, range, count or output hash changed. The first `just golden` run printed one failure, `test_run_ids_differ_by_language_and_each_is_stable`, because it read the copy before the update wrote it; the test passes on the copy that was written.
