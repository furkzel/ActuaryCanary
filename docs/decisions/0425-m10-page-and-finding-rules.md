# 0425 — The M10 page and the finding rules KF47 to KF49

## Context
M10 has a page in the report, in English and Turkish, and three rules in RP01 (1.6.0, draft, not reviewed by the owner). The page is built from the tables of the module (`report/ledger_section.py`, `templates/ledger.html.j2`), the labels are in `language.yaml` (a missing Turkish text fails a test), the section is `ledger`.

## Decisions
- **The page** has, in this order: the headline (material gaps of the entity-class-months compared, the largest with its class, entity and month, the largest unmapped share among the entities in their latest month); the profile in use with its state in words ("proposed, not confirmed", marked, or "confirmed on <date>") and the materiality; the table of classes (months compared, material gaps, largest gap and its share; a class that is not reconciled with its reason, INVESTMENT and OTHER included); the trend of the gap of each class over the months (share of the ledger amount, all entities of the class together); the gaps by month for the entity-class pairs that have a material gap (the last 18 months, a material cell marked, at most 40 pairs, the rest in `reconciliation.csv`); the 15 largest material gaps with the suggested cause and its evidence; the accounts without a class with their latest and largest balance; the profile's fingerprint, SHA-256 (first 16 digits) and rules (pattern, class, sign, balance kind). The footnote names FN01, FN02, CV12 and the files.
- **Rules.** KF47 (priority 27): at least one material gap; one sentence for the run with the number of material gaps and of the entity-class-months compared, the classes with their counts, and the largest gap with the suggested cause named as suggested and the statement that causes are not findings of fact. The rule scopes of RP01 are run, segment and line; there is no class scope and adding one is outside this item, so "a material gap per class" is one sentence that lists the classes. KF48 (28): the largest unmapped share among the entities in their latest month is at least `ledger_unmapped_share_limit`, a new setting of 0.05, the upper end of the typical range of FN02 ("after the first confirmation the share should stay near zero", 0.0 to 0.05). KF49 (29): the profile in use is proposed, not confirmed, whatever the figures say, because every figure of the page rests on it.
- **Facts** are put among the facts of the run only when M10 ran; a run without a ledger has none, so none of the three rules can fire there (the tiny baseline and its golden copies carry no sentence of them).
- **Turkish.** Labels are in `language.yaml`, the sentences of the rules in `findings.yaml`; the question of the module and the skip reason are the wording of the specification and stay English like those of the other modules.

## Consequences
- The golden copies move only by the module list and the spec versions (record 0426).
- The page by eye (light, dark, print) was not looked at; the text of the page was read from the rendered run of the tiny reinsurance_heavy scenario in both languages.
