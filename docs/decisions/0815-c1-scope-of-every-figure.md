# 0815 — C1: every table and headline of the report states its scope; M07 to M09 per segment

## Context
Release 1.3.0 added the segments on M07, M08 and M09: the headline of M07, its development charts and its all-lines delays, the headline, result and loss-ratio tables of M08 and the headline and unit costs of M09 summed LIFE and NON_LIFE, although segments are never consolidated by default (claims.yaml, MT01, MT09, RP01).

## Decision
- **Scope is declared where the page is built.** Each page body, `reserving` and the model carry `scopes`, a map from the dotted path of a table to `report.scope.declare(title, level)`. Levels: `segment` (every row names one segment; nested rows inherit it), `company` and `entity` (booked at that level by source; the page prints "booked at company level, all segments" or "by legal entity; the books carry no segment", with a note where useful) and `info` (files, rules, methods: no business figure). There is no default. `tests/report/test_scope.py` walks the whole model of the tiny baseline (two segments) and fails on a table without a declaration, a segment table whose row names no segment or two, and a company or entity table without its label; the same walk of the demo (M07 to M10, M12) is in `test_demo_report_slow.py`.
- **Fixed at the measure, not in the report.** New default views, no formula change: RC03 by segment and type; RI01 by segment and by segment and origin; RI02 by segment and calendar period; RI04 by segment and origin (`segment` is no longer "not built": claims, premium and commission are cut by segment, the reinstatement premium reaches a segment through the line it was allocated to, and a line named under two segments stops the figure with its reason); M09 EX01 and EX02 by segment and month, EX03 to EX05 by segment and period. `modules/views.py` and `measures/engine.py` were touched although outside the item's literal list: the views are where the grouping lives.
- **Pages.** M07: headline, charts and delays per segment (lines of one segment are added, segments never). M08: ceded shares, result of the last years and loss ratios with RI04 per segment; treaties, programmes, allocation and reconciliation are booked per treaty and say so. M09: headline and unit costs per segment; expenses booked without a segment are in no segment's ratio and stay in the table of expenses booked without a line, which is `company`.
- **Findings.** KF35, KF37, KF38 and KF39 stated figures added over segments: they are `segment` scope now, one sentence per segment, same conditions on segment facts (RP01 1.6.1). KF36, KF40, KF41 and KF44 to KF46 count rows, periods or treaty years, or state the share of expenses booked without a line (company level by source), and stay `run`.
- **Found by the walk and not fixed** (not the same small defect; marked `company` with a note, printed on the page): M02 cancellation and retention (PR04, PR05 on all business), M05 expense and commission ratios (EX01 to EX03 on all business; M09 cuts them), M12 headline blocks and the cash-flow table (an "All lines" row and an all-lines total). A later item should cut them or drop the totals.

## Consequences
Run ids of every example move (M07 0.1.1, M08 0.2.1, M09 0.1.1, RP01 1.6.1). `measures.csv` has extra segment views. Company-level figures of M07 to M09 are gone from the pages; the numbers per segment are the same facts cut finer.
