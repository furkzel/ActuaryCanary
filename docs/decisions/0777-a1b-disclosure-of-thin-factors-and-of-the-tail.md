# 0777 — A1b: disclosure of the factors the rule replaced and of the tail used (R08, R09)

## Context
R08: the factor 108-120 of MOTOR_TPL rests on one origin. R09: the tail of MOTOR_TPL is 1.000 while a fifth of its claims are open, and the page does not say that 1.000 is a default, not an estimate. The page already had the number of link ratios per column (table of stability), the fitted tails (MT14) with the effect of the chosen curves (`tail_effect`) and the tail used. Nothing said where the tail comes from or what the rule did to a factor.

## Decisions
- **Thin factors, placement.** Below the factor tables of the page of each line (section "Development factors and selection"): the table of stability gets a column "Note" ("set to 1.000 by the rule (one link ratio)"); under the tables one sentence per replaced factor (measure, column, number of link ratios, the average it replaced) and one sentence with the selected unpaid of the line with the averages instead of 1.000 (amount, base, signed change). Only factors the rule changed are listed: a thin column whose average is 1.000 anyway is not replaced and not named (record 0776). A column on two link ratios is in the table (the count is the column "Link ratios") and in `FEW_LINK_RATIOS`, not in `THIN_FACTOR`.
- **Where the change comes from.** A new variant `thin_average` in `sensitivity.csv` (the natural place, as `tail_fitted`): the run's methods rerun with the average entered as the selected factor of every replaced column, quantity "selected unpaid (MT10, RS02)", nominal. Unpaid, not IBNR, because the unpaid is what the review quoted (+39.8 m); the change is the same in both. Every line has a row; a line without a replaced factor has the reason "the rule replaced no development factor on this line". On the demo: MOTOR_TPL 522,419,480 with the rule, 482,641,367 with the averages (−7.6 %).
- **Diagnostic.** `THIN_FACTOR` (severity WARNING, method MT02) once per replaced column of a triangle, target the column; it replaces `FEW_LINK_RATIOS` for that column, so a column is not warned twice. Its message names the number of ratios, the limit, the average and the factor used. Both languages are not needed: diagnostics are data text (record 0224).
- **Tail box.** A block "Tail factor" on every line page, after the factor tables: a table per triangle (tail used; where it comes from: MT02 default / run setting / assumptions register with who, also when the register selected an MT14 curve; the curves MT14 fitted with their tail factors, or why not fitted); then sentences: that a default of 1.000 means no development after the last age (with the age) and is not an estimate; the share of open claims; the effect of the chosen curves on the selected IBNR (the existing `tail_effect`, same wording as the tier B block); and that the report does not choose between the tail used and the fitted curves (the tail is set in the register). The tail itself is not changed.
- **Open-claim source.** CL04 (claim counts of the run, by segment and line): open claims over claims reported, at the cut-off, over all origin years of the line (demo MOTOR_TPL: 174 of 828 = 21.0 %). If the run has no claim counts or the line none, the page says "not available" and why. It is a share of counts, not of amounts.
- **Findings (RP01 1.4.0, draft).** KF42, scope line: `thin_unpaid_change_size >= thin_factor_change_limit`, the limit **2 %** of the selected unpaid of the line (a setting of the rule file; the sentence names the replaced factors, the unpaid with the averages and the share; demo MOTOR_TPL 7.6 %). KF43, scope line: the tail is the MT02 default (`tail_default == 1`, on the selection measure) and `tail_default_concern >= 1`, that is at least one of: open-claim share **≥ 10 %**, or the fitted curves would change the selected IBNR by **≥ 5 %** in either direction. The two limits are constants of `report/thin_tail.py` (an "or" is not a clause of the rule language; thresholds sit in the code that builds the fact, like the limits of the diagnostics) and appear in the sentence through the text fact `tail_default_reasons`. 5 % of IBNR is half of KF29's 10 %: KF43 says "the default may matter", KF29 says "the fitted tail changes the reserve"; on the demo MOTOR_TPL both fire. No run-level sentence: no total is quoted, so no all-lines name is needed.
- **Specs.** MT02 1.1.0 (parameter `min_link_ratios`, the rule, outputs, diagnostic), MT06 1.2.0 (the parameter passed through), M04 1.2.0 (setting, page, sensitivity variant), RP01 1.4.0. None marked reviewed.

## Consequences
- Tests: `tests/report/test_a1b_disclosure.py` (the variant equals the run without the rule; the notes and their Turkish; THIN_FACTOR once and without FEW_LINK_RATIOS; the tail box with default, run setting, register entry; the facts), `tests/report/test_findings.py` (KF42 and KF43 fire and stay quiet on facts built by hand), the pin in `test_language.py` (40 rules, 1.4.0). The Turkish test now counts the reason of a register entry as data text.
- The sensitivity page gains one variant per line; its footnote names the variant.
