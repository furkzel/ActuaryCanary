# 0386 — M07 recoveries with RC03 and RC04: what the specifications leave open

## Context
Stage 6 of F1bc builds the measures RC03 (recovery delay) and RC04 (recovery development ratio) and the module M07 (all 0.1.0, draft, text unchanged, `implemented: true`). RC01 and RC02 exist since version 1.0 and are not changed.

## Decisions
- **One source of flows.** RC03 and RC04 read the recovery flows of `build_recoveries` (record 0384), cut at the cut-off: the bookings of the recoveries feed, or the flows derived from the to-date fields. The result says which in its notes ("source: ..."); lineage points to the rows of `recoveries` or, for derived flows, to the later valuation row of `claim_valuations`.
- **RC01 and RC02 keep their definition** (the to-date fields of claim valuations, specs 1.0.0). With a recoveries feed whose bookings differ from the to-date fields, RC01/RC02 and RC04 can therefore differ; the reconciliation table of the module (DV-RCV-08) shows by how much. Changing RC01/RC02 to the transactions would be a new version of two reviewed specs and is left to the owner.
- **RC03.** The first payment is the month end of the first valuation with `paid_to_date` above zero. The value is the mean delay in days; `n`, the median (`p50`) and the 90th percentile (`p90`, from five recoveries on, the rule of CL07) stand beside it. A booking that is not positive is a reversal, not a recovery, and is left out with a note; so is a recovery before the first payment or on a claim never paid (spec). A booking whose type has no class counts in the total and in no view by type (DV-RCV-02). Default views: by recovery type, and by segment, line and recovery type.
- **RC04.** The denominator is the cell of the paid triangle of the run (MT01), so the ratio is on the reinsurance, currency and value basis of the triangles; the numerator is the recoveries of the claims of the group and origin booked up to the end of the cell (origin start plus the age, at most the cut-off), converted and restated by `convert.bases` exactly like a to-date field of a valuation row (constant development rates on the nominal basis, CV01; rate and index of the booking month on the inflation-adjusted basis, CV02). Rows per recovery type and `TOTAL`; without a recoveries feed the total is subrogation plus salvage and says so. A view without development periods takes the latest diagonal (cumulative amounts are not added over development). A recovery on a claim without a valuation is in no origin view (DV-RCV-05) and counted in a note. Default view: segment, line, recovery type, origin and development period.
- **The module** runs RC01 to RC04 through the module runner like every module. `required_any` names `claim_valuations`, which is always loaded, so the runner asks one thing more (`PRECONDITIONS`): without a recoveries feed and without a to-date recovery column the module is skipped with the `skip_reason` of its specification, word for word. Its tables beside the measures (`modules/recoveries.py`): the flows per claim, type and month in original currency with the flag `derived`, and the reconciliation of transactions with the to-date fields (one row per difference, both values; a note says why there is none when the flows are derived or the columns are missing).
- **Ultimates gross and net of recoveries are left out.** The M07 specification lists rates, timing, development and the source with its reconciliation; it states no ultimate of recoveries and no net-of-recoveries reserve, and recoveries stay outside the reinsurance basis (CV03). Projecting RC04 to ultimate would be a method choice the specification does not make.

## Consequences
- Tests: `tests/measures/test_rc03_recovery_delay.py`, `tests/measures/test_rc04_recovery_development.py` (a hand-worked book of three claims, `recovery_fixtures.py`), `tests/modules/test_m07_recoveries.py` (both sources, the skip reason, the tables, the property that derived recoveries never exceed what was paid). Written, not run: `tests/modules/test_m07_ground_truth_slow.py` (baseline medium against `recoveries_truth.csv`).
- `tests/modules/test_runner.py` and `tests/spec/test_specs.py` list M07, RC03 and RC04 as built.
- On the tiny baseline of the generator (transactions): 15 recoveries, mean delay 94 days for salvage and 218 days for subrogation.
