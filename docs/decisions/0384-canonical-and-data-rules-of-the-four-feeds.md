# 0384 — Canonical rules, data rules and derived recoveries of the four feeds: what the specifications leave open

## Context
Stage 4 of F1bc applies DV-RTY-01..10, DV-RTR-01..11, DV-RCV-01..09 and DV-LDG-01..10 and builds the recovery flows. The specifications (all 0.1.0, draft, text unchanged) leave these points open.

## Decisions
1. **Which rules change the feed.** The canonical step (`canonical/feeds.py`) applies the rules whose action excludes or rewrites rows: DV-RCV-01..04 and DV-RTR-01..04 (presence; one row per transaction, the later source row of conflicting rows kept; labels outside the classes become UNMAPPED; an empty or invalid currency becomes UNKNOWN), DV-RTY-01, -02, -03, -08 and DV-LDG-01, -02, -04, -05 (conflicting duplicates of a treaty or ledger key are all excluded, a period that is not a month end is moved to its month end). Every other rule lists rows and leaves them in the feed (`quality/checks_feeds.py`).
2. **Treaty terms stay in the feed.** DV-RTY-04 to -07 say "excluded from allocation and what-if" (or from converted views). The row is listed and kept, so that a movement can still be matched to its treaty (DV-RTR-05, -06); the reinsurance item applies the exclusion where it allocates. The same holds for a ledger row with an invalid currency (DV-LDG-03, "excluded from reconciliation": the ledger item excludes it). The spec defaults of empty `attachment_basis`, `loss_basis` and `placed_share` are not written into the feed either; DV-RTR-06 reads an empty attachment basis as LOSSES_OCCURRING.
3. **Counts.** A rule about rows counts rows. A rule about groups counts groups and says so in its text: DV-RCV-07 claims, DV-RCV-08 valuations (claim, type and valuation date), DV-RTR-08 and -09 valuation rows of claims that have movements, DV-RTR-11 totals per type, month, line and currency, DV-LDG-08 entity-months per currency, DV-LDG-10 missing entity-months. Canonical findings list the first 20 source rows, quality findings the first 5 references (as for the six feeds); the count is complete in both.
4. **Tolerances.** Amounts are stored to the cent. Sums of bookings against a to-date field or a gross amount (DV-RCV-07, -08, DV-RTR-08, -09, -11, DV-LDG-08) agree within 0.05 of the currency unit (`AMOUNT_TOLERANCE`, the tolerance of the generator's checker); DV-LDG-06 and -07 use the 0.01 the specification states.
5. **DV-RTR-06.** A claim movement is covered when the date of loss (the policy start for a RISKS_ATTACHING treaty, when policies are loaded) lies in any contract period `[inception, expiry)` of its treaty id. A premium movement (CEDED_PREMIUM, REINSTATEMENT_PREMIUM) fails when booked before the earliest inception date of its treaty id. Movements whose treaty or claim is unknown are left to DV-RTR-05 and -07. It needs treaties and claims.
6. **DV-RTY-09.** "The same programme" is read as: excess-of-loss rows of different treaty ids on the same line, entity and loss basis whose periods overlap; their bands `[retention, retention + limit)` must not overlap (an empty limit is unlimited). Two proportional rows of the same type and different treaty ids on the same line and entity must not overlap in time.
7. **DV-LDG-07.** The opening balance is compared with the closing balance of the row before it of the same entity, account and currency, whether or not months are missing between them (a missing month then shows as a break). The exception for year-to-date income accounts needs the mapping profile (CV12), which is not built: until then an opening balance of zero in January is read as the year-start reset and is no break (the fiscal year is taken to start in January here).
8. **DV-LDG-09** needs the account-mapping profile (CV12, the ledger item). It is listed as not evaluated with that reason; nothing guesses a mapping.
9. **A second feed.** `NEEDS` names the other feeds of each cross-feed rule; when one is missing the quality report says "<feed> is not loaded" for that rule instead of the general "the fields it needs are not loaded". DV-RTR-11 is also not evaluated when premiums holds no CEDED_PREMIUM or RI_COMMISSION row (the rule starts with "when premiums also holds").
10. **Derived recoveries** (`canonical/recoveries.py`, decision 0203). `build_recoveries(feed, valuations)` returns one structure whichever the source: the columns of the recoveries feed plus `derived`. Without a feed the flows are the increments of `subrogation_to_date` and `salvage_to_date` between consecutive valuations of a claim, the first valuation counted from zero, an empty amount read as no change, a zero increment no flow, a negative increment kept as a reversal; the booking date is the later valuation date, the currency the valuation's, the id `DERIVED-<claim>-<yyyymm>-<SUB|SAL>`, and the source row that of the later valuation. An INFO finding (`CV04-DERIVED`) and the note of the structure say that the flows are derived; RECOVERY_OTHER is then not available. With a feed the transactions are used, a booking without a valid currency takes the currency of its claim (DV-RCV-03), and DV-RCV-08 lists every valuation where the bookings accumulated to its date differ from the to-date field, with both values.
11. **Module impact.** Impacts are worked out by the quality engine from the fields the measures and methods of the built modules use. A rule on a field that only M08 or M10 will use shows no impact until that module is built; the specifications are not changed for it.

## Measured on the generated data (size tiny, checked 2026-11-11)
The rules agree with the generator's own checker (`check_phase2.py`) on every count it produces: DV-RTR-10 9 (reinsurance_heavy 17, demo 3), DV-LDG-07 1 (demo 4; the row after the missing row of the truth file `ledger_differences.csv`, kind `missing_row`), DV-LDG-08 1, DV-RTR-08 1 and DV-RTR-09 1 in reinsurance_heavy, every other rule 0. One finding goes beyond the checker: **DV-RTR-06 lists 54 premium movements in baseline (37 in demo, 122 in reinsurance_heavy)** booked in 2021, before the first contract period the treaties file holds (2022-01-01). The checker implements only the claim half of the rule. The movements of the warm-up year are in the transactions file while the treaty rows of that year are not, so the finding is a true property of the generated data; whether the generator should write the earlier contract period is an owner decision for a generator item (not changed here).
